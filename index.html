Our Approach

Every child is a wall we help build, one brick at a time.

Our namesake reminds us that a wall is only as strong as what holds it together. Five developmental bricks make up our whole-child framework — laid, like real brickwork, so each one supports the next.

Heart

Kindness and emotional wellbeing, built through morning huddles and gentle, consistent care.

Mind

Curiosity and early literacy and numeracy, explored through daily learning labs.

Hands

Creativity and innovation, practised freely at open-ended play stations.

Body

Nutrition, rest and outdoor play, balanced across every full day.

Friends

Social skills built in a small, close-knit classroom where every child is known by name.

Frequently Asked

Questions parents ask us most.

Can't find your answer here? Call us and we'll walk you through it.

What age can my child join, and how does admission work?

We welcome children from 6 months (Infant Care) through age 5. Little ones enter our 2–4's program based on their birth year. Enquire by phone, book a visit to meet the lead teacher for your child's age group, then complete the enrollment form to confirm your seat.

What is the teacher-to-child ratio in each classroom?

We keep classrooms small by design — roughly 1 teacher for every 4 children in Infant Care, 1 for every 6 in Toddlers, and 1 for every 10 in Preschool, so every child gets noticed, not just supervised.

What's the difference between Half Day, Full Day and Extended Day?

Half Day covers morning huddle through lunch. Full Day adds learning labs, rest and outdoor play through mid-afternoon. Extended Day extends care into the early evening for working families — ask us for current timings.

Are meals and snacks provided?

Yes — nutritious, age-appropriate meals and snacks are part of every Full Day and Extended Day schedule, prepared with mealtime routines that build healthy habits early.

How do I book a visit before enrolling?

Call us at +91-96099 05596 or use the Book a Visit button on this page. We'll set up a twenty-minute tour of the classroom for your child's age group.
